<TosSection id="short-version" icon="circle-info" title="The Short Version" group="privacy" open>

- I don't use advertising, social media trackers, or Google Analytics.
- My site analytics are anonymous and cookieless. They show me which pages and buttons get used, not who used them.
- If you send me a message or a commission request, I keep what you sent so I can reply and do the work.
- I never sell your information.

</TosSection>

<TosSection id="who-i-am" icon="user" title="Who I Am" group="privacy">

I'm Jess, the artist and developer behind KokoNekoArt (kokoneko.art), based in Manitoba, Canada. I'm the person responsible for the information described here. You can reach me through the [contact form](/contact).

</TosSection>

<TosSection id="what-i-collect" icon="database" title="What I Collect" group="privacy">

### When you send me a message

The contact form asks for your name, how you'd like me to reply (a platform username or an email address), an optional subject, and your message. I use this only to answer you.

### When you request a commission

The commission request form collects your email address, the details of your request, any reference images you upload, and the options you picked (which decide the price). I use this to price the order, take payment, and make your art. Your reference images are sent to me by email and are not published anywhere.

### When you pay

Payment happens through PayPal. I never see or store your card or bank details. PayPal tells me that the payment went through and gives me an order number and the email address on your PayPal account, so I can match the payment to your request.

### Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as your IP address, the page requested and the time) to run and secure the service.

</TosSection>

<TosSection id="analytics" icon="chart-simple" title="Anonymous Analytics" group="privacy">

I use <ExternalLink href="https://umami.is">Umami</ExternalLink>, a privacy-focused analytics service. It sets no cookies and does not build a profile of you or follow you to other sites. For each visit it records things like:

- the page you viewed and the site you came from
- your browser, operating system, device type, screen size and language
- your approximate country
- simple actions: which social link, portfolio piece or commission button was clicked, which section of the home page was scrolled into view, and how far through a form people get before finishing or leaving
- how long people spend with the commission calculator, and which options they end up on (not every click)

For forms it records only that a form was started, finished, or left, and the names of fields that had a problem (for example "email"). It never records what you typed. Your IP address is used to work out the country and is not stored. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, the analytics script doesn't run for you.

</TosSection>

<TosSection id="spam-protection" icon="shield-halved" title="Spam Protection" group="privacy">

The contact and commission forms use Google reCAPTCHA v3 to tell people from bots. It loads when you start using a form, runs in the background, and is subject to <ExternalLink href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">Google's Privacy Policy</ExternalLink> and <ExternalLink href="https://policies.google.com/terms">Terms of Service</ExternalLink>. Google may use cookies and information about your device and browsing behaviour for this purpose. Messages that look like bots may be discarded.

</TosSection>

<TosSection id="cookies" icon="cookie-bite" title="Cookies and Local Storage" group="privacy">

kokoneko.art doesn't set cookies of its own. Your reduced-motion choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it, and it never leaves your device. PayPal and Google reCAPTCHA may set their own cookies when you use the checkout or a form.

</TosSection>

<TosSection id="other-services" icon="share-nodes" title="Who Else Handles Your Information" group="privacy">

I use a few services to run the site. They process data for me, and only for these purposes:

- **Vercel**, for hosting
- **Resend**, for delivering the emails that your form submissions generate to me. Resend keeps a log of those emails, which can include your message, your email address and any reference images, for a limited time.
- **Google reCAPTCHA**, for spam protection
- **PayPal**, for payments
- **Umami**, for anonymous analytics

Some of these companies are based in the United States, so information may be stored or processed outside Canada.

</TosSection>

<TosSection id="retention" icon="clock" title="How Long I Keep It" group="privacy">

I keep messages and commission details for as long as I need them to reply and complete your commission, and afterwards for my own business and tax records. Email delivery logs are also kept by Resend for the period set by its service, separately from my own copy. Anonymous analytics don't identify anyone, so they are kept as long as they're useful to me.

</TosSection>

<TosSection id="your-rights" icon="scale-balanced" title="Your Choices and Rights" group="privacy">

You can ask me at any time to see the information I hold about you, correct it, or delete it (I may need to keep payment records for tax and legal reasons). You can also withdraw your consent to my keeping your information. Send me a message through the [contact form](/contact) and I'll respond as soon as I can.

If you're not happy with how I've handled your information, you can contact the <ExternalLink href="https://www.priv.gc.ca">Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada</ExternalLink>.

</TosSection>

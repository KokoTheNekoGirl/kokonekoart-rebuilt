  - title: Inspiration
    content: |
      For my app, I went for Disney NOW. It focuses on what is currently live on cable TV. It would know the TV timeslots as you are required to link your cable provider to watch certain things on the app. Some are free though. Disney has a few different channel slots on TV, including Disney Junior, Disney Channel, and Disney XD. Disney Channel also has TV Original movies in addition to Disney NOW hosting games as well.

      I decided to combine all of this into a kid-friendly mobile app that currently doesn't exist. I also decided to rebrand Disney NOW to replicate more of a professional yet child-like aesthetic. Using colours found in Disney media, I found a beautiful sunset palette to use for this project. I wanted to reference the layout for Disney Plus as parents using the app will be able to navigate easily. Still different, but the baseline is there.
    images:
      - src: "@portfolio/streamer-inspo.png"
        alt: "The moodboard with the sunset colour palette I used for the app"

  - title: Process
    content: |
      I made the buttons big for a reason. Kids love pressing buttons and especially in younger children, they recognize images before they recognize words. While yes, some words are required for the older audiences of the app, I intentionally hid that in the scroll for the tablet design. Kids usually use tablets for their own navigation while parents use their phones to put the media on it for them.

      I made a distinction for the media with the icons on the home page categories. Why? Personally, there's nothing I hate more than trying to find a movie to watch only to find out it's a 7 season show. I thought to make it apparent and clear before you press it, especially if games are included in the app too. That's another thing, I added two different medias in this app: Shorts and Games. Shorts are usually only 2-5 minutes long while TV Shows are 20+ minutes long. I thought this distinction was also important. Additionally, porting all web games into the app would be fun too! It keeps the kids entertained in different ways.

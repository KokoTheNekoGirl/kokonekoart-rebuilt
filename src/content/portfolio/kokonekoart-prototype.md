slides:
  - title: Context
    content: |
      During college, we were tasked with designing and developing a 4-pager site with animations using [LottieDocs](https://lottiefiles.github.io/lottie-docs/) and [FullPageJS](https://alvarotrigo.com/fullPage/). By the end of the project, we were to host it on a live domain. I decided to attempt to develop a commission site for myself, as Ko-fi wasn't meeting my needs. I made a change to my commission structure to try and make it more clear how I priced my work, but Ko-Fi's structure wasn't built for gradual commission prices. Therefore, I knew I wanted to create radio-like button groups to calculate the prices of artwork, and potentially also show an example with the selected options.
    images:
      - src: "@portfolio/kokonekoart-beta-context.png"
        alt: "A screenshot of how Ko-fi add-ons are set up."

  - title: Inspiration
    content: |
      My inspiration comes from paper scraps, sketchy doodles, and stop motion paper animation. Here is the mood board I created specifically for this project.

      There were also a couple of paper animations I was inspired by from [Damita_K.N](https://www.youtube.com/@Damita_K.N) on YouTube. The specific videos I drew inspiration from were [Peppermint](https://youtu.be/hoYxHBlT-4I) and [Doctor](https://youtu.be/JJxflcxRkhI). These two videos are considered [animation memes](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=animation%20meme), which is a really cool community in general!
    images:
      - src: "@portfolio/kokonekoart-beta-inspo.png"
        alt: "Mood board of paper scraps, sketchy doodles and paper textures"

  - title: Process
    content: |
      I started off with a mock-up of the desired design in Figma. I had a pretty clear vision in mind, utilizing the artsy, paper aesthetic with the graphical elements I sourced. I did create original assets for nearly half of the project, this being all 12 possible combinations of art references and the 2 animated SVGs. I used preexisting art for my art gallery. I then started testing the button functionality in a local instance. Once I knew it would work, I began developing the site proper.
    images:
      - src: "@portfolio/kokonekoart-beta-process.png"
        alt: "First interation of the Figma mock-up."

  - title: User Research
    content: |
      In my UI class, we could pick any project to conduct a user research study on. I picked this specific project to gather feedback to improve on the design further. The testers offered good feedback, which translated to better site structure and leaning more into the paper aesthetic. I also had the commission prices in a local instance, so I also gatchered feedback on how they functioned as well.

      You can see the redesign in comparison to the original in the Figma file linked on the final slide.
    images:
      - src: "@portfolio/kokonekoart-beta-research.png"
        alt: "The redesign I went with after user feedback"

  - title: Ultimate Decision
    content: |
      Creating this project was intended to be my commission site with this very domain. However, later down the line I decided to combine my digital art business with my web developer freelancing works despite the advisory of industry professionals. While I'd love to have two, segregated sites dedicated to the two aspects of myself from a business standpoint, at the end of the day hosting and maintenance costs are much too high to justify the separation. I'm also loud and proud of who I am and what I do, so I'm willing to show that in a merged space with a brand that brings softness and comfort.

      You can find out more of the design process of the current version of this site [here](/portfolio/kokonekoart).

  - title: Context
    content: |
      During college, we were tasked with redesigning a local business to be a mobile responsive 3 pager site with fake product mock-ups. I chose the local sandwich shop [Hoagie Boyz](https://www.hoagieboyz.com/), as I knew I could make the “bad on purpose” look intentional but also still a good design overall.
    images:
      - src: "@portfolio/hoagie-boyz-context.png"
        alt: "The original Hoagie Boyz website"

  - title: Inspiration
    content: |
      At the time, I was listening to alternative and punk rock music with my partner. So, I really wanted to bring that vibe into this mock up. Punk culture has a unique style as its ideals stem from anarchy. It defies the laws of society and doesn't care what it has to say. I think that's what Hoagie Boiz was initially trying to go for. Albeit more “memeified”, as it feels bad on purpose. Just because something is bad intentionally, doesn't make it good. It's a delicate balance. To break the principles of design, you need to fully understand what, where, and when you can break them. This way, going for the grunge aesthetic works.

      Other local websites I took inspiration from were [Smoke's Poutinerie](https://smokespoutinerie.com/) and [Dreamland Diner](https://www.dreamlanddiner.com/).
    images:
      - src: "@portfolio/hoagie-boyz-inspo.png"
        alt: "Inspiration collage with the color pallette"

  - title: Process
    content: |
      I knew the kind of layout feel I wanted, so instead of my usual process of creating wireframes, I decided to wing it and dive in head first into the mock-ups. This way, I could keep the flexible document flow while also adjusting to maintain design principles.

      I'm happy with what the final product came out to be. The addition of all the collage-type images with the screen tone style really gives a creative voice to this local business. I think this design choice really brings out my intention. I love the added detail of the animated images having a 2-frame animation to give a comic-book feel. A challenge I faced was to ensure the absolute positioned elements remained mobile responsive. I also had a lot of fun with the menu too, ensuring the columns all collapsed on mobile was very satisfying.
    images:

        alt: "Desktop view of the redesign on the menu page"
